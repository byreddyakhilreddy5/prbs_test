First Output After Seed Load:
   - After loading the seed, the first output is NOT simply the MSB 8 bits of the seed
   - The first prbs_out must be generated by shifting the LFSR 8 times starting from the loaded seed value
   - This matches the testbench behavior and is critical for correct operation

Seed Preprocessing:
   - Before storing the seed into the LFSR register, the seed must be preprocessed through 8 iterations of the PRBS feedback polynomial

Generating prbs_out from Loaded Seed:
   - After the seed is loaded into the LFSR, the first prbs_out is generated by shifting the LFSR 8 times starting from that loaded seed value
   - This means: starting from the loaded LFSR state, perform 8 shift operations (each shift applies the PRBS feedback polynomial) and extract 8 bits to form the 8-bit output
   - The MSB of prbs_out corresponds to the first bit extracted from the LFSR (before any shifts)
   - Each subsequent bit of prbs_out comes from the LFSR after each shift operation
   - IMPORTANT: The LFSR must be shifted exactly 8 times to generate the first prbs_out, as this matches the testbench expectations
